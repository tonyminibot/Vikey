Changelog

1.0.29 · 01/11/2026

  • Typing style setting: Telex, Simple Telex 1 or Simple Telex 2 (under Vietnamese typing).
  • Excluded apps (Mac): in the apps you pick, Vikey leaves your keys alone. The list starts with remote desktop apps like Jump Desktop, TeamViewer and AnyDesk.
  • Turn auto switch on or off for each app, right from the menu bar (Mac).
  • Typing a word, a punctuation mark, Delete, then more letters no longer loses the word before it ("vieejt." Delete "nam").

1.0.28 · 31/10/2026

  • A shorter main window: Auto switch, Vietnamese typing, Emoji panel and General now fold. A folded section still shows what is on (Mac).
  • Teencode: common chat words like "zui zẻ", "zậy", "gòy", "thík" stay as typed instead of turning back into keys. English words like "zero" and "look" are left alone. Turn it off under Vietnamese typing (Mac).
  • Typing "(" or "." right after a space no longer changes the word before it.
  • Windows: fixed a crash that could happen when closing the settings window.

1.0.27 · 30/10/2026

  • "os" in a Vietnamese sentence gives "ó" ("la với ó").
  • Switch language, type a word, switch back, then Delete: no longer edits the word before it ("xin hello" → "xin heín").
  • Delete a letter with a tone and type it again: "có" → Delete → "o" gives "co", not "coo".
  • ← then → back to the same spot in a word: Vikey still knows the word ("th" ← → "if" gives "thì").
  • No more deleted space before a word: "ử -" and "ís" + w.
  • Very long words (over 32 keys) no longer lose their first letter or get typed twice.

1.0.26 · 30/10/2026

  • Windows and Linux now have everything the Mac app has: the emoji picker (search in Vietnamese, English or Telex), a first-run guide, the new switch sound, the Acknowledgements window.
  • Windows and Linux type like the Mac: keep typing after Delete undoes a fix ("vaay" → Delete → "j" gives "vậy"), numbers glued to words ("5trăm"), looking at the next word ("vào office ít nhất").
  • Linux: checks for a new version every day and tells you.
  • Android: closer to the iPhone keyboard: suggestions pick on lift, frequently used emoji, emoji search with :D, text replacements (omw), app language, clearer dark mode.

1.0.15 · 29/10/2026

  • Keep typing after Delete undoes a fix: "vaay" → Delete → add "j" gives "vậy".
  • Numbers glued to words: "5trawm" gives "5trăm", "mua 1casi" gives "1cái"; "100W" stays W.

  • Fewer manual switches with English mixed in: "test có work", "em sẽ optimize".
  • New switch sound: rising into Vietnamese, falling into English.

1.0.14 · 29/10/2026

  • Smarter auto-switch for words that are both English and Vietnamese: Vikey also looks at the word before ("cho con bú" but "đi xe bus", "ở bên" but "it's been").
  • Built from over 180 million words of Vietnamese and English text: fewer wrong words and fewer manual switches.
  • "ạ" right after an English word no longer becomes "aj".
  • Vikey for Windows: first release, same typing engine and auto-switch as the Mac app (x64 and ARM64).
  • Vikey for Linux: first release, an IBus engine for Ubuntu/Debian (.deb) and Fedora (.rpm), x64 and ARM64.

1.0.13 · 29/10/2026

1.0.0 · 28/10/2026

The first version.

  • Auto-switch English/Vietnamese as you type: in English mode "treen" gives "trên", in Vietnamese mode "more" still gives "more".
  • Fixes the earlier word too once the sentence is clearly English: "thí makes sense" becomes "this makes sense".
  • Leaves programming words and variable names alone: var, const, git push, obj.dir, my_var.
  • Press Delete right after Vikey fixes a word to undo it.
  • Remembers the language for each app and each website.
  • Works with Dvorak and Colemak keyboards. Steps aside when you switch to another input method such as Japanese or Chinese.
